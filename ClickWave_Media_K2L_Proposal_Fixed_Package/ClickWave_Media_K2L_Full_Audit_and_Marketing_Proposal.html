07. Terms, Responsibilities & Measurement

Ad spend: Meta advertising budget is not included in the monthly service fee and is paid separately by K2L directly to Meta.
Follower target: 1,000 new Facebook Page followers per month is a campaign target, not a guaranteed outcome. Results depend on budget, audience response and platform delivery.
Reviews: Review growth will focus on inviting real customers to leave honest feedback. No fake or purchased reviews will be used.
Production expenses: Professional photography, external videography, models, travel and third-party production costs are excluded unless agreed separately.
Scope: Additional work outside the selected package may be quoted separately. Content volume is managed within the agreed plan and reasonable capacity.
Client support: K2L will provide accurate service information, timely approvals, platform access and a contact person for customer inquiries requiring business decisions.
Measurement: Monthly reporting may include published content, reach, engagement, video views, profile activity, inquiries where measurable and ad results where applicable.
No guarantee: Sales, leads, reach, followers, reviews and platform growth cannot be guaranteed. Strategy and optimisation will be based on observed performance.
